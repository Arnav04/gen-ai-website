Background

Why does this matter?

When AI generates a "software engineer" or "housekeeper," who does it picture? These defaults shape how people see professional roles — and who feels like they belong in them.

Prior research shows Image search results for jobs like "programmer" or "engineer" have long underrepresented women and minorities — shaping who imagines those careers as attainable.
The shift to text generation LLMs are now used to generate personas for product design, hiring simulations, and synthetic datasets — making their demographic defaults matter more than ever.

"Biased" AI is not a new issue. It's an inherited one.
